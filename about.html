01WHO WE ARE

A local builder with a national reach.

Registered in Nigeria and grown out of an established oil & gas parent company, Mitsab operates where engineering discipline meets local knowledge of how projects actually get built.

Mitsab Resources Global Limited is a multi-disciplinary Nigerian company delivering projects across construction, exploration and environmental services. We were established under the company that birthed us, T-SAB Resources, an established name in oil & gas — which means our roots are technical, not just administrative.

That background shapes how we work: clear scopes, tracked schedules, and a project team that stays close to the client from planning through handover. We take on the projects that need someone who understands both the engineering and the ground conditions.

Our subsidiary, De-Absareal Global Limited, extends that same discipline into scaffold installation and tank maintenance — the kind of industrial upkeep work that keeps a facility running safely between major projects.
