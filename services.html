02CAPABILITIES

Five disciplines, one project team.

From first survey to final handover, these are the services we bring in-house — plus the subsidiary that carries our name onto the tank farm and the scaffold.

General Contracting

End-to-end oversight of a construction project — subcontractor hiring, budget, scheduling and specification compliance.

Project & Program Management

Planning, coordination and oversight so projects finish on time, within budget and to the required quality standard.

Manpower Supply & Management

Personnel and technical resourcing for oil & gas and industrial clients, backed by relevant regulatory partnerships.

Renovation & Remodeling

Updating and improving existing structures — from minor repairs to major overhauls, residential through industrial.

Minor Civil Maintenance

Tailored solutions for infrastructure upkeep, including road repairs and sidewalk maintenance.

Welding & Fabrication

Structural steel, piping, supports and custom metalwork, built for durability and adherence to safety standards.

Pre-Construction Services

Feasibility studies, cost estimation and risk assessment that surface challenges before construction begins.

Borehole Drilling Services

Reliable, sustainable water solutions for residential, commercial and agricultural needs.

Earthwork & Landscaping

Grading, excavation and outdoor space design that balances practical land development with aesthetics.

CAT Engine Service & Maintenance

Specialized servicing, repairs and routine maintenance for CAT engines and heavy-duty equipment.

Marine Transportation Services

Safe, reliable boat transport for passengers, goods and equipment across nearby waterways.

03HOW WE WORK

The same four stages, every project.

Repeatable process is how projects stay on schedule and on budget. This is the sequence our clients can expect.

STAGE 01

Plan

Scope, schedule and resource plan built around your objectives, before ground is broken.

STAGE 02

Mobilize

Personnel, equipment and documentation staged so execution starts without delay.

STAGE 03

Execute

Work tracked against the plan, with deviations flagged and corrected early, not late.

STAGE 04

Handover

Clear closeout, documentation and a client relationship built for the next project.
